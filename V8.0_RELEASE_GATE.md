## Current limitations

The available market history is consensus closing probabilities/lines, not verified executable prices from individual bookmakers. The public multi-provider parquet audit returned HTTP 403. No bookmaker rows have been imported. CLV and real-price ROI are therefore not established.

### 2025-26 same-sample benchmark (1,131 games)

| Market | AI Brier | Market Brier | AI log loss | Market log loss | AI directional accuracy | Market directional accuracy |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Moneyline | 0.211245 | 0.199059 | 0.610654 | 0.581550 | 67.46% | 68.44% |
| Spread | 0.272196 | 0.250333 | 0.743038 | 0.693814 | 49.87% | 49.51% |
| Totals | 0.279904 | 0.249803 | 0.813335 | 0.692752 | 48.72% | 51.02% |

The AI's spread directional accuracy is marginally higher, but its Brier score, log loss and calibration error are materially worse. For moneyline and totals, the market also has better directional accuracy. These results do **not** establish a betting edge; probability quality and executable prices matter more than headline hit rate.

### Market-aware hybrid experiment (same 2025-26 holdout)

A regularized hybrid using the market close plus a pregame Elo/score-edge feature was also tested on the same 1,131-game holdout:

| Market | Raw market Brier | Hybrid Brier | Raw market log loss | Hybrid log loss | Raw market accuracy | Hybrid accuracy |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Moneyline | 0.199059 | 0.199244 | 0.581550 | 0.581985 | 68.44% | 69.05% |
| Spread | 0.250333 | 0.249986 | 0.693814 | 0.693119 | 49.51% | 50.93% |
| Totals | 0.249803 | 0.251052 | 0.692752 | 0.695256 | 51.02% | 49.07% |

The hybrid produces a small improvement on spread probability scores in this single holdout, but not on moneyline or totals. This is exploratory and requires independent seasons, bookmaker prices, CLV and net-ROI validation before any release decision.
