## Actual source schema inspected

The pinned SBR NBA archive schema (from the upstream scraper's `NBAOddsScraper` class) contains:

- `season`, `date`, `home_team`, `away_team`
- `home_final`, `away_final`
- `home_close_ml`, `away_close_ml`
- `home_open_spread`, `away_open_spread`
- `home_close_spread`, `away_close_spread`
- `open_over_under`, `close_over_under`

It does **not** contain both-side spread prices or both-side totals prices. Therefore it can support historical market-line benchmarks and moneyline price tests after validation, but cannot by itself support realistic spread/total ROI.

The upstream repository software is MIT-licensed, but the redistribution rights of its bundled historical data have not been independently established. Keep the archive in temporary CI cache; do not publish or redistribute the raw data without rights review.

## Correction committed

The audit now:
- Selects canonical database team names rather than internal team IDs.
- Maps SBR mascot names to canonical NBA team names.
- Retains a strict same-home/same-away team join and ±1 local-date candidate window.
- Preserves score validation and ambiguity reporting.
