## 4. Important game-table coverage discrepancy

The historical games table currently has 1,322 rows for 2021-22, 1,319 for 2022-23 and 1,318 for 2023-24. The earlier Phase 0 reference counts were 1,323, 1,320 and 1,319 respectively. That is one fewer game in each season than the reference counts. This needs to be reconciled before declaring full market coverage. Do not manufacture replacement rows.

## 6. Interpretation guardrail

No backtest or ROI result should be reported as a V8.0 success until the game universe and market coverage are reconciled. The closing market is a benchmark, not a guarantee of a profitable betting strategy. Opening and closing line values can only be assessed when the underlying source genuinely contains both observations.
