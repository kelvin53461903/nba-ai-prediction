## Release decision

Overall research gate: FAIL. None of the three markets passes the full release gate.

- Moneyline: market baseline remains stronger on the final holdout.
- Spread: no demonstrated edge over market probabilities.
- Totals: no demonstrated edge over market probabilities.
- ROI is not calculable from fair probabilities/fair odds. The source does not provide executable two-way spread/total prices.
- CLV is not calculable because executable two-way opening/closing prices with timestamps are unavailable.
- No player injury/availability features are present.

## Additional pre-tip leakage audit

The earlier Phase 6C consensus-line artifact contained 7,577 rows. A strict timestamp audit found 6,411 rows whose recorded snapshot was at or after scheduled tip-off. These rows were excluded before pregame feature generation. Only 1,166 strict pre-tip rows remained. On that filtered dataset, Phase 7B still failed both Spread and Totals release gates.

This strict filter is deliberately conservative: a line with no verified pre-tip timestamp is not treated as a valid pregame input.
