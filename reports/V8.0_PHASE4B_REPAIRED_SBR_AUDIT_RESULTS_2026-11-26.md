## Why Phase 3F's earlier rejection was not the final word

The first audit did not apply two documented upstream corrections to the SBR archive:
1. In approximately 7% of rows, closing spread and total fields are swapped; the home spread sign can be recovered from the two closing moneylines.
2. The 2019-20 bubble dates are one year early (not part of the two seasons audited in this run, but included in the reusable parser).

The correction logic was independently inspected in the MIT-licensed `llimllib/nba_data` repository, `pipeline/odds_archives.py`, and its tests in `tests/pipeline/test_odds_archives.py`. This project has a separate implementation and 9 dedicated unit tests.

## Acceptance status: REVIEW_REQUIRED

The archive is now a materially better candidate for a limited historical closing-line benchmark, but it is not accepted for full three-market ROI backtesting:
- SBR has closing moneyline prices for both teams.
- SBR does not provide both-side spread prices or both-side over/under prices in this archive. Therefore spread and total ROI cannot be calculated honestly.
- 12 spread rows and 65 total rows remain unavailable/invalid and must remain NULL, not imputed.
- This is closing-line history, not opening-line history; it cannot support opening-to-close CLV.
- Moneyline Brier is a market-baseline diagnostic on these same games, not a model's out-of-sample performance.
