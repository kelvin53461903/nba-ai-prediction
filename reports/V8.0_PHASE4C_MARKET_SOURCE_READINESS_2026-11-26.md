## Executive decision

A new candidate archive, The Lines Monitor NBA Data Health page, reports a free six-season archive (2020-21 through 2025-26) covering all 7,776 games with a closing spread and total, and 7,774 with a closing moneyline. It says the data is assembled from three sources:
- Consensus spread/total: CC0.
- BetMGM closing lines: CC BY-SA 4.0.
- OddsPortal average moneyline: MIT.

The page reports its own game matching and final-score validation, but the exact raw downloadable file URLs, immutable source revisions and row-level schemas have not yet been verified independently in this phase. Therefore this is a **candidate source**, not yet approved for import. No data was copied from the displayed summary.

Public source: https://thefantasymonitor.com/lines/

These limitations mean it cannot establish historical opener-to-close CLV or Pinnacle/Circa close performance.

Current stored market rows:
- SBR: 2,426 rows (1,146 + 1,280), with spread, total and closing moneyline fields.
- Consensus closes: 2,445 rows (1,314 + 1,131), with opening/closing line and consensus probability fields.
- 2022-23 and 2023-24 currently have no stored market rows.
- The 2025-26 game-results table is intentionally cut off at 2026-04-02 UTC and does not represent the complete 2025-26 season.

The source page's season game counts differ from the current database by one game in 2021-22, 2022-23 and 2023-24. Those three game-table gaps must be reconciled against the official game schedule before calling coverage complete. The 2025-26 table is cut off earlier and must not be compared as if it were a full-season universe.

## Other sources reviewed

- SportsDataverse / hoopR: consensus closing spread and total from 2020 bubble through 2026. A related source review reports a usable archive, but its repository currently has no explicit license declaration (NOASSERTION); do not ingest into a distributable/commercial project until license permission is clarified.
- The Odds API: historical NBA coverage begins in 2020, but retrieving historical snapshots requires a paid plan. Coverage discovery is available on all plans; actual historical odds retrieval is not free.
- HoopsAPI: past-season closing odds downloads are part of a paid Data plan. Not a free backfill solution.
- The 2025-26 Hugging Face sample is CC BY 4.0, but overlaps data already represented in the current market table and is not a six-season backfill.

## Import decision

**NOT APPROVED YET.** Do not import the candidate 7,776-game summary until:
1. Obtain the exact raw consensus, BetMGM and OddsPortal files and stable source revisions.
2. Verify each source's license file and attribution obligations, including whether redistribution into this repository is permitted.
3. Confirm schema, source-specific meaning, home/away sign conventions, line/price fields and settlement rules.
4. Independently match by teams and date to the existing game table, then require exact final-score agreement.
5. Keep source rows separate. Do not silently blend consensus, BetMGM and OddsPortal or relabel a consensus line as Pinnacle.
6. Preserve all unconfirmed/conflict flags and missing moneyline rows.
7. Dry-run duplicates, orphans, score mismatches, season coverage and market ranges on the isolated database before any insert.
8. Keep ROI disabled for spread/total unless both-side prices are present. A closing line without both prices is suitable as a line benchmark, not a real-money ROI calculation.
