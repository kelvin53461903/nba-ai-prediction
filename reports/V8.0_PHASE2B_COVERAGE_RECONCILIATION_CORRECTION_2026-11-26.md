## Correction

The prior audit compared total season rows against reference counts that were not established as authoritative. It therefore incorrectly described the 2021-22, 2022-23 and 2023-24 game tables as each missing one game.

The season-type breakdown shows why the totals differ:

| Season | Regular season | Play-in / playoffs / cup final | Total in DB |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 2020-21 | 1,080 | 91 playoffs | 1,171 |
| 2021-22 | 1,230 | 92 playoffs | 1,322 |
| 2022-23 | 1,230 | 89 playoffs (including play-in) | 1,319 |
| 2023-24 | 1,230 | 87 playoffs + 1 In-Season Tournament Final | 1,318 |
| 2024-25 | 1,237 | 84 playoffs | 1,321 |
| 2025-26 to 2026-04-02 UTC cutoff | 1,138 | 0 in this extract | 1,138 |

The row counts are internally plausible given the stored season_type labels. There is no proven one-game discrepancy for those three seasons. The earlier claim is withdrawn. The 2024-25 regular-season count includes the NBA Cup final classification used in the current source, and should be interpreted from the stored season_type column rather than compared to a simplistic 1,230-game regular-season expectation.

## Source access and ingestion gate

The Lines Monitor data-health page reports a 2020-21 to 2025-26 archive with closing spread and total for 7,776 games, and closing moneyline for 7,774. It explicitly reports no opening lines or quote timestamps. It describes the source licences as CC0 (consensus spread/total), CC BY-SA 4.0 (BetMGM) and MIT (OddsPortal average).

Public page: https://thefantasymonitor.com/lines/

However, a public data-health page is not itself a raw downloadable data artifact. The exact downloadable file URLs, schemas, immutable revisions and row-level licence/source fields have not yet been pinned and inspected in this repository. Therefore do not import based solely on the headline coverage claim.
