## Executive decision

The SBR archive audited in Phase 3F remains quarantined. A more promising free-source candidate has been identified: The Lines Monitor's published Data Health report describes a six-season NBA closing-line collection assembled from three datasets with stated open licences.

Candidate page: https://thefantasymonitor.com/lines/

This is a lead for independent verification, not a passed V8.0 source. The published figures belong to the publisher's own audit and must not be represented as our independently reproduced results.

## Publisher-reported coverage

The Lines Monitor reports:
- 7,776 games, 2020-21 through 2025-26.
- 7,776 games with closing spread and total.
- 7,774 games with usable moneyline.
- 7,776 games with play-by-play.
- 7,230 regular-season games, 36 play-in, 507 playoff games and 3 NBA Cup finals.
- Every market row is joined by same-day team pair and checked against NBA.com final score.
- Eight spreads remain flagged as unconfirmed; two playoff games have no usable moneyline.

Published source composition:
1. Consensus closing lines — CC0; spreads and totals for all games; moneylines through January 2023.
2. BetMGM closing lines — CC BY-SA 4.0; spread, total and moneyline with prices and bet shares, October 2021 to February 2026.
3. OddsPortal average across books — MIT; closing moneyline across seasons.

Published limitations:
- No historical opening lines or line-movement timestamps.
- The collection is not a Pinnacle/Circa sharp closing benchmark.
- The publisher flags a period of unreliable consensus spreads from 2025-12-15 to 2026-01-04.

## Independent acceptance gates before use in V8.0

Do not import anything until all of these are completed:

1. Obtain exact original dataset URLs, immutable versions/commits and file checksums.
2. Inspect each dataset's actual licence file/metadata, attribution requirements and redistribution terms. A website's description alone is not sufficient legal clearance.
3. Download the raw files in a read-only audit workflow; record byte count, SHA-256, row count, schema and date coverage.
4. Independently match game date and both teams to the isolated NBA game table.
5. Verify source final scores exactly against the isolated NBA results; never repair a score mismatch.
6. Validate spread sign convention and home/away orientation against moneyline direction without using final results to choose the line.
7. Validate totals distribution, over/under settlement balance and line-to-final-total residuals by season.
8. Validate moneyline prices, two-sided completeness, de-vigged probabilities, Brier and log loss.
9. Keep missing and conflicting rows visible; do not silently fill or discard them.
10. Keep a source-level provenance column and source-specific quality status for every market record.

## Import policy

- Neon remains read-only during this discovery and audit stage.
- No writes to production Supabase or the formal NBA prediction history.
- No source is allowed to pass merely because it has broad coverage.
- Spread/total ROI cannot be computed without valid two-sided prices for those markets.
- If the original raw files and immutable source identifiers cannot be recovered, this candidate is not importable.
