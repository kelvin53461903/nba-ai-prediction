## 2. What failed

The transformed SBR market values remain unsuitable despite applying the upstream spread/total swap rule.

Diagnostics from the read-only run:
- Moneyline: 2,490 pairs; direction accuracy 52.09%; Brier 0.289012. This is not credible as a closing market benchmark and is rejected.
- Spread: 2,491 usable lines after the upstream swap repair; still requires independent source validation.
- Total: 2,492 usable lines; mean close total 193.969 vs mean actual total 222.211; over results 2,242, under 243, pushes 7 (89.97% over). This is a severe inconsistency and is rejected.
- The sample includes values where the raw spread/total columns are swapped; the repair restores some, but the totals still fail the distribution checks.

Decision: do not use SBR's pre-scraped transformed market fields for AI backtesting, market calibration, ROI or CLV. Keep only the verified team/date/score mapping evidence. No data has been imported into Neon.

- 7,776 NBA games from 2020-21 through 2025-26.

The data-health page separately identifies the CC0 consensus archive, MIT OddsPortal moneyline archive and CC BY-SA BetMGM archive.
